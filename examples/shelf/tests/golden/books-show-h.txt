Show the books with the given ids, in the order given

Usage: shelf books show [OPTIONS] <ID>...

Arguments:
  <ID>...  The ids of the books to show, separated by spaces

Options:
  -h, --help  Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  shelf books show 2 5

Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --long, --full. `shelf books show --help` describes them.
