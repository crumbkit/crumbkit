Describe the books with the given ids, or the whole catalogue, in one sentence

Usage: shelf books summarize [OPTIONS] [ID]...

Arguments:
  [ID]...
          The ids of the books to describe, separated by spaces; without ids, the whole catalogue

Options:
      --json
          Print the result as a JSON document on one line

      --fields <FIELD>
          Print only these comma-separated fields, in this order

      --long
          Add the long columns after the compact ones

      --full
          With --json, print every field of every row

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Examples:
  shelf books summarize
          Summarize the whole catalogue

  shelf books summarize 2 5
          Summarize books 2 and 5
