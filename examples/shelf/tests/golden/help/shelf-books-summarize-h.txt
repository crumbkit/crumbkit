Describe the books with the given ids, or the whole catalogue, in one sentence

Usage: shelf books summarize [OPTIONS] [ID]...

Arguments:
  [ID]...  The ids of the books to describe, separated by spaces; without ids, the whole catalogue

Options:
  -h, --help  Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  shelf books summarize
  shelf books summarize 2 5

Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --long, --full. `shelf books summarize --help` describes them.
