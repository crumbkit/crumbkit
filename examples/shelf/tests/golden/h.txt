Browse a small catalogue of public-domain books

Usage: shelf [OPTIONS] <COMMAND>

Browse the catalogue
  books  Work with the books in the catalogue

Options:
  -h, --help     Print help (see more with '--help')
  -V, --version  Print version

Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --long, --full. `shelf --help` describes them.
Topics: catalogue. `shelf help <TOPIC>` prints one.
